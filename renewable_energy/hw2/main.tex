\begin{letterenum}
\item The instantaneous power is given by the equation
\begin{equation}
    P = \frac{1}{2} \rho A U^3 C_P
\end{equation}
where $\rho$ is the air density, $A$ is the swept area,
$U$ is the wind speed, and $C_P$ is the power coefficient.
We assume the air is at STP and therefore has a density of
$1.225$ kg/m$^3$. We compute swept area by first converting
to meters
\begin{equation}
    A = \frac{\pi}{4} D^2 = 2339 \text{m/s}
\end{equation}
We also convert the wind speed to m/s before performing the computation
in Python.  Table (\ref{table:powers}) shows the instantaneous power as a function of
time.
