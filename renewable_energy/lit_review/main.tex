\title{Frequency Control for Inverter-based Resources}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
    Increased penetration of Renewable Energy Resources (RES) is rapidly changing
    the dynamics of the power grid as inverter-based resources (IBRs) have dramatically
    different physical characterstics to synchronous generators (SGs), the grid's
    conventional power source.
    Short time-scale frequency control is a vital aspect of maintaining grid
    reliability and has been the topic of renewed research interest due to these
    changes. We provide a review of which compares and contrasts conventional
    frequency control of SGs with the control of grids with high IBR
    penetrations, with a focus on grid-forming controls. We also review
    the current status of commericialization for grid-forming technologies.
\end{abstract}
