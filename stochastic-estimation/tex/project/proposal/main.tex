For the project, I will develop a model for Li-ion battery degradation which
predicts the state of health (SoH) -- a proxy for the remaining lifetime of the battery -- as a function
of voltage, current, and temperature during charge/discharge. I will analyze publicly-available experimental
datasets and utitlize the Kalman filtering (KF) methodology from the course plan to obtain a
real-time prediction algorithm as the end goal. In general, this problem is complex and nonlinear so
I anticipate an extended Kalman filter will be necessary, but it may also be possible to linearize the problem
by extracting domain-specific features.

A work plan (with rough time estimates) follows:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Literature review and access datasets (1 day at 4-5 hr work/day)
\item Preliminary analysis of data (2 days)
\item Implement and validate KF (2 days)
\item Synthesize and present results (2 days)
\end{enumerate}
